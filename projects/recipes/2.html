Mac and Cheese

<- Back to the recipe book

This mac and cheese recipe has been used over and over, time and time again throughout my entire lifetime. It's your everyday mac and cheese, but with just one small extra tang. And somehow, that makes it one hundred times better. My mother is always the one who makes it, and whenever she does, you can almost see the joy and anticipation radiating off of her. My father absolutely adores this recipe, and he was ecstatic to tell me:
I love this recipe because it tastes good. Also because it means I don't have to cook dinner.
-JH
What a moving quote. Anyway, back to business. On this page, I will tell you how to DIY this delicious recipe and enjoy it with your family, friends, strangers, pets, monsters under the bed, or by yourself if you're feeling like a hermit. It makes quite a bit, so you'll likely have leftovers for days!

Ingredients Recipe
  • 3 cups grated cheddar, Gruyère, Swiss, or a combination
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 pound cut pasta (e.g., elbows)
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • Pepper
  • 1/2 cip bread crumbs
  1. Bring a stockpot of water to a boil and salt it.
  2. When the water boils, add the pasta and stir occasionally. Start testing after 5 minutes.
  3. When the pasta is barely tender, drain it and reserve some cooking water. Return the pasta to the pot.
  4. Add the grated cheese, milk, butter, and a sprinkle of salt and pepper. Stir until the cheese melts, adding cooking water a tablespoon at a time to thin if necessary. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
  5. Transfer the pasta to a 9x13 in. baking dish. Sprinkle the Parmesan and bread crumbs over the top.
  6. Broil until the cheese is bubbly and brown and the bread crumbs crisp, 2 to 5 minutes. Serve.
Makes about 8 servings.
Tips, Tricks, and & Substitutions
  • Instead of getting the fancy cheeses yourself, you can probably find a good blend of cheese at a local grocery store. Kraft's Mac and Cheese Mix (Not sponsored) is our go-to option.
  • Through experience, we have found that 1/2 cups of Parmesan instead of 1 tastes better, because the cheese flavor tends to get very overwhelming.
